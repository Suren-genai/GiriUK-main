## **1\. Project Overview**

GIRI UK is an ecommerce platform offering a wide range of traditional Indian products, devotional items, books, puja essentials, idols, handicrafts, dancewear, jewellery, home decor, gifts and textiles.

The objective of this project is to revamp the existing GIRI UK website into a modern, visually appealing and user-friendly ecommerce platform while retaining its traditional Indian identity.

The redesigned website will provide customers with a smooth shopping experience, easy product discovery, convenient navigation, secure checkout and improved access to customer services.

The website will be designed to serve customers across the UK and other supported international markets.

The project will cover:

* Complete UI/UX redesign of the website.  
* Responsive ecommerce website development.  
* Product catalogue and category management.  
* Advanced product search and discovery.  
* Shopping cart and checkout.  
* Customer registration and account management.  
* Payment gateway integration.  
* Order tracking and customer notifications.  
* Content and blog management.  
* Ecommerce administration and reporting.

## **2\. Project Objectives**

The key objectives are:

1. Transform the existing website into a premium traditional Indian ecommerce shopping experience.  
2. Improve the presentation of products and categories.  
3. Make product discovery easier through search, navigation and filtering.  
4. Provide a simple and secure shopping and checkout process.  
5. Improve customer engagement through promotions, reviews and loyalty-related content.  
6. Support mobile, tablet and desktop users.  
7. Provide an administration interface to manage products, categories, orders and website content.  
8. Establish a scalable ecommerce platform that can support future business growth.
