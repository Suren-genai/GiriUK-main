GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Delivery & Returns Inquiries

Everything you need to know about receiving and returning your devotional treasures.

What happens if my Brass Idol or Diya arrives damaged? expand_more

In the rare event of transit shock or damage, photograph the item alongside its packaging and email us at support@giriuk.com or WhatsApp our London helpline at +44 20 8903 8920 within 48 hours. We dispatch an immediate priority replacement free of charge and provide instructions for the respectful surrender of the broken deity under our sacred protocol.

Do I need to pay customs duties or import taxes in the EU? expand_more

No. For all European orders under €150 delivered to Germany, France, Netherlands, Ireland, and across the EU, VAT is settled at checkout via our EU IOSS registration. You will not face unexpected courier clearance fees or domestic post office holding taxes when your parcel arrives.

Can I collect my order in person from your Wembley hub? expand_more

Yes! We offer a complimentary "Click & Collect" service at our Wembley Cultural Sanctorum (HA9 7ET). Simply choose 'Collect at Wembley' at checkout. Orders placed before 1:00 PM are typically ready for collection on the same afternoon between 3:00 PM and 6:30 PM.

How do I track my shipment once it has left your logistics center? expand_more

As soon as your shipment is scanned by Royal Mail or DPD, you will receive an automatic dispatch notification containing a live 13-character tracking number (e.g. GB293848209RM) and an estimated 2-hour delivery window. You can also monitor your parcel anytime through our top header "Track Order" link.

Are books and dance jewellery eligible for return if sizing is wrong? expand_more

Absolutely. Books with crisp, uncreased spines and Bharatanatyam dance ornaments (belts, ghungroos, temple necklaces) can be returned within 14 days, provided they have not been worn for public performances and retain all original velvet tags and pouches.
