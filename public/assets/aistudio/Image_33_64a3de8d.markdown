## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, culturally rooted luxury boutique tailored for South Asian heritage goods, sacred literature, handcrafted brass idols, and spiritual lifestyle essentials within the UK and European market. The emotional tone blends deep spiritual reverence with contemporary British e-commerce fluency: authoritative, authentic, warm, and impeccably curated.

The aesthetic fuses classical editorial elegance with modern transactional clarity:
- **Palette and Tone:** Grounded in a rich forest heritage green, warm natural ivory surfaces, and antique brass/gold accents that honor temple metalwork and sanctified ritual crafts.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, sacred, inviting, and uncluttered. High-contrast imagery featuring warm illumination (diya lamps, brass work, jasmine flowers) is set against generous ivory negative space rather than cold clinical white.
- **Design Philosophy:** Tactile heritage luxury meets frictionless shopping. Information density is disciplined, typography is expressive yet legible, and every interactive touchpoint provides reassuring confirmation.

## Colors

The color palette centers around the sacred triad of forest deep teal-green, antique brass gold, and warm ivory paper:

- **Primary (`#164E45`):** Deep heritage forest green. Used for authoritative structural surfaces, header announcement banners, primary CTAs, active states, and grounding hero elements.
- **Secondary (`#B99A5B`):** Antique temple gold. Applied as an accent tone for eyebrow borders, decorative filigree rules, coupon highlight badges, star ratings, and subtle gilded highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`):** Bright ceremonial brass. Reserved for interactive focus rings, hover embellishments, and promotional accents on deep backgrounds.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F4ED`):** Warm unbleached ivory parchment. Forms the primary canvas background, creating warmth and softening image transitions compared to pure stark white.
- **Supporting Tones:**
  - **In-Stock Status Indicator:** Emerald green (`#1B8754`) accompanied by an organic pulse indicator.
  - **Text Ink Primary:** Charcoal soot (`#1A2421`) ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against ivory surfaces.
  - **Text Ink Muted:** Earthy sepia slate (`#5C6863`) for secondary specifications, metadata, and breadcrumbs.
  - **Surface Elevation Cards:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle warm borders (`#EBE6DC`).

## Typography

The typographical architecture unites literary poise with clean e-commerce legibility:

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Evokes timeless Sanskrit and classical Indian literature while delivering the polish of an upscale Mayfair or Kensington boutique. Headings utilize graceful italic variants for lyrical storytelling phrases (e.g. *the Beauty of Indian Traditions*) and standard roman weights for structured departmental catalog headers.
- **Body & Navigation (`Manrope`):** A modern geometric sans-serif balancing human warmth with structural clarity. Its high x-height and open apertures maintain optimal legibility for British pound currency pricing, complex spiritual terminology, and SKU specifications across screen resolutions.
- **Eyebrow Tags:** Rendered in uppercase `Manrope` with wide tracking (`0.14em`), bracketed with delicate antique gold rule lines (`#B99A5B`) to establish editorial pacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt baseline rhythm within a 12-column responsive fluid grid:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max-width container of `1320px` centered on the viewport. 12 columns with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and `48px` (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `16px` gutters, and `24px` margins. Product grids collapse from 4 columns to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `16px` gutters and `16px` margins. Horizontal product cards switch to compact 2-across grids or single-row carousel cards with peek overflow.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major thematic blocks (Curated New Arrivals, Customer Favourites, Heritage Journal) maintain consistent `space-2xl` vertical padding, demarcated with centered antique gold dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation employs subtle ambient illumination instead of heavy dropshadows, evoking the gentle, warm glow of brass oil lamps:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat warm ivory (`#F7F4ED`) base background.
- **Level 1 (Product Cards & Testimonials):** Solid white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #ECE7DE`) and soft warm ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(22, 78, 69, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(185, 154, 91, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover State & Search Bar):** Floated card state: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(22, 78, 69, 0.09), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(185, 154, 91, 0.08)`. The primary search bar sits elevated with an pill-profile ambient halo.
- **Level 3 (Navigation Flyouts & Modals):** Crisp white background with `0 20px 38px -6px rgba(22, 78, 69, 0.16)` and a gilded 1px gold top accent boundary.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced rounded geometry (Base Level 2: `0.5rem` / `8px`):

- **Product & Category Cards:** Styled with `8px` (`rounded-md`) corners to present brass icons, books, and handloom silks with soft, tailored framing.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Primary and secondary action buttons adopt elongated pill contours (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an inviting, approachable tactile quality that contrasts the formal serif typography.
- **Search & Input Bars:** Pill-contoured text inputs with inset icons for an open, seamless search experience.
- **Review and Testimonial Cards:** Subtly rounded at `12px` (`rounded-lg`) for a soft, comfortable reading card container.

## Components

### Header & Announcement Bar
- **Announcement Strip:** Heritage green background (`#164E45`), centered layout with gold coupon tag icons, promo code pill highlight (`GIRIUK2026` in `#D4AF37`), divider rules, and UK free-delivery threshold notices.
- **Utility Header:** Ivory canvas containing high-resolution heritage identity, co-branding mark (*In UK & EU by SHIVAYAA*), expanded pill search field with subtle magnifying icon, and right-aligned user actions (*Track Order*, *Login*, *Wishlist*, *Cart with gold count badge*).
- **Primary Category Navigation:** Clean horizontal bar featuring bold category headings (*Festival Special*, *New Arrivals*, *Books*, *Puja*, *Idols & Handicrafts*, *Dancewear & Jewellery*, *Home & Gifts*, *Textiles*) with directional chevron carets.

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Cart Action:** Deep green pill button (`#164E45`) with white text and cart iconography. Hover activates a gentle lift and brass border outline.
- **Secondary Exploration Action:** Transparent pill button with forest green border (`1.5px solid #164E45`) or gold directional inline arrow (`Explore →`).
- **Wishlist Floating Button:** Circular white icon pill (`36px × 36px`) anchored to the top-right of image viewports with subtle shadow and heart outline.

### Product Card
- **Structure:** Crisp white surface with image window framed in warm beige, high-resolution product photography, title in `Manrope` regular, GBP price in bold (`£XX.XX`), and a green status dot with "● In Stock" label.
- **Button Placement:** Full-width deep green pill button `Add to Cart` resting securely at the card footer.

### Value Propositions & Trust Bar
- **Badges:** Fine gold outline icons (Lotus, Shield, Van, Headset, Trustpilot Star) set within warm circular frames, accompanied by concise title and reassurance subtitles (*Authentic Indian Products*, *Secure Payments*, *UK Delivery*, *Customer Support*).

### Newsletter Footer
- **Form Bar:** Inset ivory input box paired with an attached or contiguous deep forest green pill submission button displaying an arrow icon.
- **Footer Base:** Deep forest green canvas with gold section headings, warm off-white navigation links, social pill icons, and UK/EU payment badge verifications (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay).
