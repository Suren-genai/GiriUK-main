Murtis, Consecration & Mandir Protocols

However, true Prana Pratishtha (infusing cosmic life energy) is sacredly intended to occur within your own home mandir by your family or family priest. With every vigraham purchase, GIRI UK provides a printed booklet with step-by-step simple home consecration mantras in Sanskrit with Roman transliteration.

To preserve the sacred luster of authentic Indian brass (Pithalai) and Panchaloha without scraping the intricate chisel details:

  1. Traditional Method: Soak tamarind pulp in lukewarm water, rub gently onto the brass surface, let stand for 2 minutes, and rinse thoroughly with clean warm water. Dry immediately with an untreated cotton muslin cloth.
  2. Pitambari Powder: Wet the metal lightly, apply authentic Pitambari paste with your fingers (avoid abrasive wire sponges), rinse, and buff with a soft microfiber towel.
  3. Avoid Harsh Chemical Polishes: Never apply automotive polishes or bleach-based bathroom cleaners to sacred deities.

Each item listing clearly specifies the sacred metallurgy:

Virgin Brass (Peetal) Solid, high-density alloy of copper and zinc, lustrous golden finish, ideal for daily home archana.
Antique Bronze (Kansa) Heavier alloy of copper and tin with rich patina, classical temple aesthetic with deep acoustic resonance.
